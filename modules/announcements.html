VOIDNEXUS Module

Announcements

College-wide and departmental announcements.

Description

The Announcements module lets admins and faculty publish official notices to the whole campus or to specific departments, courses, or roles. Students see them in the VOIDNEXUS app and can get optional push or email notifications. Keeps everyone aligned without relying on notice boards or scattered emails.

Features

  • → College-wide and departmental targeting
  • → Role- and course-based audiences
  • → Rich text and optional attachments
  • → Scheduling and expiry
  • → Delivery via app, push, and email

Use Cases

Admin notices

Holidays, fee deadlines, and policy updates.

Department news

Course changes, lab timings, and department events.

Emergency alerts

Campus-wide or building-specific alerts.

Event promotion

Drive visibility for campus and inter-college events.

Integration

Announcements use VOIDNEXUS identity and roles for targeting. They appear in the same feed as events and can be linked from the Events module. Works with Campus Messaging for follow-up discussion and with the full platform for a single student experience.
